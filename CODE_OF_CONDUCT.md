# Conduct

Treat people respectfully and discuss technical evidence rather than personal attributes. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing and unwanted sexual content are unacceptable in project spaces.

The maintainer reviews reports and may request corrections, remove inappropriate content or restrict participation proportionally. No independent moderation board or guaranteed response time is implied. A confidential community-reporting route must be verified before public opening; do not post sensitive reports to public threads.
